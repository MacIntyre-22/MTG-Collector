## Description

Cardhold is the fast, beautiful way to browse, organize, and price your Magic: The Gathering collection. Point your camera at a card and Cardhold identifies the exact printing instantly. No account, no clutter, and your collection stays on your device.

POINT AND SCAN
Hold a card in frame and Cardhold reads it live, locking onto the right card in a fraction of a second. It reads the set and collector number to pin the exact printing, even on older cards and special art, then lets you add it to any binder or deck with a tap.

SEARCH EVERY CARD
Search the entire Magic database, powered by Scryfall. Filter by colour, type, set, format legality, mana value, and price, sort however you like, and page through thousands of results.

DAILY DISCOVERY
The Home tab surfaces a fresh set of cards every day: popular staples, pricey chase cards, budget picks, stunning full art, and the newest releases.

ORGANIZE YOUR WAY
Sort your cards into binders and decks, with a catch-all collection for everything you own. See your whole collection's value, card count, and colour breakdown at a glance.

LIVE PRICES
Up-to-date prices in USD, EUR, or MTGO tix, shown everywhere from card details to binder and deck totals. Choose your currency once and it flows through the entire app.

CARD DETAILS AND RULINGS
Crisp card images, full oracle text, set symbols, legality by format, and official rulings for the cards that need them.

CARDHOLD PRO
Unlock the full toolkit:
- Unlimited binders and decks
- Detailed binder and deck stats, including mana curve and type breakdown
- iCloud sync across your devices
- Unlimited card scanning
- Deck import from plain text and CSV
- Export and share your binders and decks with a link

Free to start: search, card details, daily discovery, your main collection, and a couple of card scans a day are always free.

Card data, images, and prices are provided by Scryfall. Cardhold is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast. Magic: The Gathering is a trademark of Wizards of the Coast LLC.

## 10-Screenshot Shot List

Capture these on a real device (the scanner needs a camera, and your own binders/decks make the
collection screens look real). Order them so the first 2-3 sell the app on the listing page. Suggested
caption overlay text is in quotes.

1. **Live card scanner** (camera framing a card, ring locking on) - "Scan a card. Get the exact printing."
2. **Scan result sheet** (resolved card with price + add button) - "The right printing, in a snap."
3. **Home / Daily Discovery** (suggestion rows) - "A fresh set of cards every day."
4. **Search results grid** (a colourful search) - "Search every Magic card."
5. **Filter sheet** (colours, type, set, format) - "Filter by colour, type, set, format, and price."
6. **Card detail** (image + prices + rulings) - "Full details and live prices."
7. **Binder view** (grid of your cards) - "Organize your collection into binders."
8. **Deck view** (a deck with boards) - "Build and track your decks."
9. **Deck or binder stats** (value, mana curve, breakdown) - "Know your value, colours, and curve."
10. **Share / iCloud sync** (share sheet or sync settings, or the Pro screen) - "Sync across devices. Share with a tap."

Required sizes: 6.9" and 6.5" iPhone are mandatory; add 5.5" if you support older devices. The same
images can be reused across iPad if you list it.
