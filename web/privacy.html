Privacy Policy

Cardhold · Last updated 17 June 2026

Cardhold is an app for browsing and organizing Magic: The Gathering cards. It has no user accounts, and it is built to keep your data on your own device and in your own iCloud. This policy explains what happens with information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Your collection data — the binders, decks, cards, notes, and settings you create — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your collection data is stored in your private iCloud account so it can sync across your devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access the contents of your private iCloud data.

Camera (card scanner)

The card scanner uses your device camera to recognize card names. This processing happens entirely on your device. Cardhold does not store, upload, or transmit camera images.

Shared collections

When you choose to share a deck or binder, Cardhold uploads a snapshot of that collection — the card list, quantities, the collection's name and notes, and the cover image you set — to a public Apple CloudKit database in order to generate a shareable link. This only happens for collections you explicitly share.

Anyone who has the link can view and import that shared collection. Shared snapshots are one-time copies and do not update if you later change the original. Only share collections you are comfortable making accessible to anyone with the link.

Third-party services

Card data, images, and prices are provided by the Scryfall API. The app also loads MTG news from public RSS feeds (such as MTGGoldfish, EDHREC, and Reddit) and fetches site logos for outbound links from public icon services. These requests send only what any web request includes (for example your device's IP address and the site being looked up) and do not include your personal or collection data. In-app purchases are handled by Apple; Cardhold never sees your payment information.

Children's privacy

Cardhold is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your data & deletion

You can remove your on-device data at any time from the app's settings, or by deleting the app. Disabling iCloud sync, or deleting the app on all your devices and removing its data from your iCloud settings, removes the synced copy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
