Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought Cardhold Pro with.

My collection isn't syncing between devices.
iCloud sync needs to be enabled in the app's settings, and each device must be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on. Sync can take a moment after changes.

How do I import or export a deck or binder?
Open a deck or binder, tap the share icon, and choose Export File (text or CSV) or Import Cards. Plain-text decklists and CSV files from common sites are supported.

How does sharing a collection work?
Tap the share icon on a deck or binder and choose Share Link. Anyone who opens the link on a device with Cardhold installed gets their own copy. See our Privacy Policy for what a shared link contains.

How do I delete my data?
Use the data option in the app's settings to remove your on-device collection, or delete the app. If you used iCloud sync, removing the app from all devices and clearing its iCloud data removes the synced copy.
